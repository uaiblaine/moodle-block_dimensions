Pseudo-link card: only the title is a real <a class="stretched-link">, whose ::after overlay makes the whole card clickable. That keeps the link's accessible name short (just the competency name) and lets the star stay a sibling control instead of an interactive nested inside an anchor.
WCAG 1.1.1 split: the gradient and its halftone are decorative and aria-hidden; a real photo is role="img" with the competency name as aria-label. Tags sit in a role="group" labelled by the cardtags string. The access pill is aria-hidden + pointer-events:none — pure affordance.
A competency only earns a card when it has at least one visible linked course, and repeats across plans are de-duplicated (get_eligible_competency_ids / skip_competency_for_visibility). This card carries no trail and no count text — it is the plan card minus everything below the title.

Dark mode touches nothing here, and that is the design. The card background, border, title, focus outline, star platter, empty star and every shadow are single declarations naming surface, line, ink, focus-ring, ink-muted and shadow; 31 of the 34 tokens are chains onto core's own --bs-* names, so this whole column is what core's [data-bs-theme="dark"] block makes them. The three exceptions — shadow, scrim and favourite — are the entire contents of the plugin's one activation rule. The decorative gradient does move, because its stops are tone tokens (warning-edge → favourite → warning-ink) rather than literals; the halftone and an admin-configured fill or chip colour do not, because --dimension-custombgcolor and --dimension-customtextcolor are instance data the mode layer is forbidden to declare in either mode.
Same WCAG contract as light: decorative gradient aria-hidden, photo role="img" + name, tags in a labelled role="group", the star a sibling button with aria-pressed, only the title a real link.
Same eligibility rule: one card per competency that has a visible linked course, de-duplicated across plans; no trail, no count text.
